Jumper is designed to make moving and trading assets across chains efficient — including the fees you pay to use it.

The Jumper fee depends on the product you're using and, for standard swaps and bridges, the type of assets involved.

This page covers the fees charged by **Jumper itself**. Network gas, price impact and fees charged by underlying bridges, DEXs, issuers or other providers may also apply depending on the transaction.

<Note>
  Jumper always shows your route and expected output before you confirm a transaction. Where applicable, additional network and provider costs are included in the transaction details.
</Note>

## Jumper fees at a glance

| Product | Jumper fee |
| --- | --: |
| Main — Stable swap | **0 bps (0%)** |
| Main — Major swap | **2 bps (0.02%)** |
| Main — Other swap | **5 bps (0.05%)** |
| Main — Stable bridge | **1 bp (0.01%)** |
| Main — Major bridge | **2 bps (0.02%)** |
| Main — Other bridge | **5 bps (0.05%)** |
| Jumper RWA - All transactions | **5 bps (0.05%)** |

## Understanding basis points

Jumper fees are displayed in **basis points (bps)**.

A basis point is one hundredth of a percentage point:

- **1 bp = 0.01%**
- **2 bps = 0.02%**
- **5 bps = 0.05%**
- **25 bps = 0.25%**
- **85 bps = 0.85%**

For example, a 5 bps Jumper fee on a \$1,000 trade is **\$0.50**.

## Main Swap fees

Fees on standard Jumper swaps depend on the assets being traded.

| Asset category | Jumper fee |
| --- | --: |
| Stable | **0 bps (0%)** |
| Major | **2 bps (0.02%)** |
| Other | **5 bps (0.05%)** |

### Stable

Stable-to-stable swaps have a **0 bps Jumper fee**.

### Major

Major-to-major and stable-to-major swaps have a **2 bps (0.02%) Jumper fee**.

### Other

Other-to-other, other-to-major and other-to-stable swaps have a **5 bps (0.05%) Jumper fee**.

## Main Bridge fees

Fees on standard Jumper bridge transactions also depend on the assets involved.

| Asset category | Jumper fee |
| --- | --: |
| Stable | **1 bp (0.01%)** |
| Major | **2 bps (0.02%)** |
| Other | **5 bps (0.05%)** |

These are Jumper's fees for using the route. The underlying bridge or network may introduce additional costs, which can vary depending on the route and current network conditions.

## RWA fees

Jumper charges a flat **5 bps (0.05%) fee on all trades made through Jumper RWA**.

The same Jumper fee applies across supported RWA assets and routes.

Other costs may apply depending on the tokenized asset, issuer, network and underlying route.

## Other costs you may see

The Jumper fee is only one component of the total cost of an onchain transaction.

Depending on your route, you may also see:

### Network fees

Blockchain transactions require gas to be processed by the network.

Network fees vary by chain and current demand and are paid to the network rather than Jumper.

### Bridge and provider fees

Some bridges, DEXs, liquidity providers or other protocols used in a route may charge their own fees.

These depend on the provider and route selected.

### Price impact

A trade can move the market price, particularly when trading a large amount or an asset with limited liquidity.

Price impact is not a Jumper fee. It reflects the available liquidity for the trade.

### Slippage

The market price can change between requesting a quote and executing a transaction.

Slippage tolerance determines how much movement you are willing to accept before the transaction fails. Slippage is not a fee charged by Jumper.

## Check before you sign

Jumper searches across available routes and providers to find an efficient path for your transaction.

Before confirming, review the route, expected output and transaction details shown in Jumper.

That way, you're comparing the actual outcome of the transaction — not just one fee in isolation.
