Jumper RWA is a dedicated surface for discovering and trading **tokenized real-world assets** — including tokenized stocks and curated asset baskets — across multiple chains.

It brings together assets from several issuers into one place, so you can see the onchain equities market at a glance, explore individual assets, compare how the same stock is tokenized by different providers, and trade through Jumper.

Market data is aggregated from Jumper, DeFiLlama and CoinGecko and refreshes roughly every 30 minutes.

<Note>
  Coverage currently spans approximately 40 tokenized stocks that meet a minimum bar of **\$1M onchain market cap** and **\$1K daily volume**, sourced from issuers including Ondo, xStocks, Robinhood, Coinbase and bStocks.
</Note>

## Fees

Jumper charges a **5 bps (0.05%) fee on all trades made through Jumper RWA**. The same Jumper fee applies across all supported RWA assets and routes.

For a complete breakdown of Jumper fees across all products, see [Fees](/fees).

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard is the home of Jumper RWA and the fastest way to explore the tokenized-stock market.

Every listed asset appears in a sortable table with its symbol and name, issuer, current price, 24-hour change, 7-day performance, onchain market cap and 24-hour trading volume.

You can narrow the list using filters for issuer, onchain market cap and 24-hour volume, and page through the available assets.

Above the table, three curated sections surface different parts of the market:

- **Top Gainers** — the strongest 24-hour performers
- **Top Stocks** — the largest names by combined onchain market cap
- **Trending** — assets seeing the strongest momentum, up or down

Together, they give you a quick view of what is happening across the tokenized-stock market and make it easy to jump directly to the assets you're interested in.

## Asset page

Selecting any asset opens its dedicated page — a full profile of that tokenized stock.

At the top you'll find the essentials:

- Name and ticker
- Issuer
- Supported chains
- Current price
- 24-hour performance
- 7-day performance
- Onchain market cap
- Trading volume

A price chart with selectable timeframes shows how the asset has traded over time.

The asset page is also where you can trade.

An embedded Jumper trade widget comes pre-configured with the asset as the destination token, allowing you to swap into it from a stablecoin or another supported token and, where required, move funds across chains as part of the same flow.

Alongside the trade panel, information about the underlying real-world asset and the issuer behind the token provides additional context about what you're trading.

## Issuers & comparison

The same underlying stock can be tokenized by more than one issuer.

Those tokenized versions can differ in meaningful ways, including:

- Supported chains
- Fee structure
- Available liquidity
- Redemption mechanics
- Other issuer-specific terms

The issuers section makes those differences visible, listing the providers behind the available assets and providing context on each.

The comparison view builds on this by allowing you to compare different tokenized versions of the same underlying equity side by side.

This makes it easier to understand how the available versions differ and choose the one that best fits your needs — whether that's its available liquidity, supported chain, fee structure or issuer.

<Note>
  Tokenized real-world assets are issued by third parties. Availability, eligibility, redemption rights and other terms can differ between issuers, assets and jurisdictions. Review the relevant issuer terms before trading.
</Note>

## Asset baskets

Asset Baskets let you access curated, **auto-rebalancing baskets of tokenized real-world assets**.

Instead of buying and managing each underlying asset individually, you can deposit into a basket and hold a diversified position through a single strategy.

The Asset Baskets section lets you browse the available baskets and explore the strategy behind each one.

Each basket has its own dedicated page where you can review details including:

- The basket's strategy
- Its current asset composition
- How the allocation is structured
- Your position
- Your performance over time

Because baskets rebalance automatically, their composition can adjust over time without requiring you to manually manage each underlying asset.

Asset Baskets make it easier to get exposure to a group of tokenized real-world assets without having to build and maintain the portfolio yourself.
