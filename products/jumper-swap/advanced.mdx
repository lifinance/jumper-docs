Jumper Advanced is the power-user surface for trading on Jumper. Alongside the standard swap, it adds order types and modes built for larger sizes, messier portfolios and more precise intentions — so you can express exactly what you want to do, and let Jumper handle the execution across chains and venues.

It lives at `jumper.xyz/advanced` and shares the same routing and liquidity engine as the rest of Jumper, with more control over how a trade is shaped and executed.

<Note>
  Advanced is organized around a **Swap** and a **Bridge** tab, plus dedicated order modes — **Limit orders**, **TWAP**, **Multi-swap** and the **Exact-output flow**. Modes you use most can be pinned as favorites so they show up first.
</Note>

## Fees

Jumper currently charges **0 bps (0%) in Jumper fees** when using Jumper Advanced.

Network gas costs and fees charged by underlying protocols or liquidity providers may still apply. Any applicable costs are shown as part of the route before you confirm a transaction.

## Advanced swap

The Advanced swap is the same trade you know, with the routing made transparent and configurable.

Jumper aggregates liquidity across multiple DEXs and aggregators, then verifies and compares the returned quotes so you're routed through the option that actually gives you the best outcome — not simply the first quote returned.

You stay in control of the details: choose your slippage manually on any route, or let **Auto** set it for you based on the asset's volatility, and hand-pick which providers you want to route through.

You see the route, expected output and key parameters before you commit, and Jumper's execution flow carries the trade through approval, signing and settlement with clear status at each step.

### Jumper Flow

For larger trades, **Jumper Flow** splits a single large order into multiple smaller orders.

Large orders can consume available liquidity and move the price against you. By breaking an order up, Jumper can source better rates across venues and reduce that impact, which makes Flow particularly useful for larger trades.

You get a better effective price on size without having to manually split the order and place each piece yourself.

## Advanced bridge

Advanced Bridge brings the same routing intelligence to moving assets between chains, with the same hands-on control.

Instead of manually choosing a bridge, Jumper aggregates bridges and cross-chain routes, verifies their quotes and lets you steer the outcome.

You can choose the specific **provider** you want and select the **route type** — for example, fastest or cheapest — depending on what matters for that transaction.

You can also swap and bridge in a single flow when your input and destination tokens differ.

### Smart deposits

Advanced Bridge also gives you access to **smart deposits**.

A bridge that would normally require several separate transactions can be collapsed into a single action. This removes much of the multi-step, multi-signature work involved in moving funds across chains while keeping the route transparent before you sign.

## Limit orders

Limit orders let you set the exact price you want to trade at and wait for the market to come to you instead of taking the current market price.

The mode pairs order placement with a **price chart** synced to the asset you're pricing, so you can review recent price action and set a target.

Under the hood, Jumper aggregates multiple limit-order providers, such as CoW, 1inch and Velora, and routes the order across available venues.

Once placed, an order remains open until it is filled, cancelled or expires, with its current status visible in Jumper.

## TWAP

TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) lets you execute an order in several smaller chunks over time instead of all at once.

For larger trades, a single market order can move the price against you and increase slippage. Splitting the trade into scheduled slices can reduce market impact and smooth the average execution price.

Set the total amount and let the order execute progressively without having to manually place each individual swap.

## Multi-swap

Multi-swap lets you sell multiple tokens at once and consolidate them into a single output token in one flow.

Instead of approving and swapping every asset individually, select the tokens you want to sell, choose the asset you want to receive and Jumper handles the batch.

It's useful for cleaning up a portfolio, consolidating dust or rotating several positions into a single asset.

## Exact-output flow

The exact-output flow flips the usual swap around.

Instead of starting with the amount you want to spend, enter the token and exact amount you want to receive. Then choose the token you want to pay with and Jumper works backwards to calculate the required input.

This is useful when you need to reach an exact balance or acquire a specific quantity of an asset without trial-and-error on the input amount.
